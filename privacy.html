Privacy Policy - Cortes e Clipes

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. What we access

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the app receives basic profile information (display name and avatar) and permission to upload and publish videos to that account.

2. How we use it

This access is used only to identify the connected account and to send or publish the owner's own videos to it. We do not use it for any other purpose.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. Authentication is handled through TikTok Login and the integration provider Composio.

4. Storage

We do not keep a database of personal data. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok account settings under connected apps.
